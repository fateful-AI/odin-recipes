Fried- Rice-Recipe

Nigerian Fried Rice is a vibrant, deeply savory dish celebrated for its signature yellow hue, crunchy vegetable mix, and rich flavor profile built entirely upon a base of heavily seasoned chicken or turkey stock. Unlike regular fried rice, this version is distinctively seasoned with curry powder and thyme, then loaded with a colorful medley of carrots, green beans, sweet corn, peas, and green bell peppers, alongside small prawns and finely diced beef or chicken liver. To achieve its perfect, non-sticky texture, the starch-free parboiled rice is cooked in the stock until just tender, then stir-fried in small batches over high heat with the vegetables and proteins, resulting in a beautifully toasted, fragrant meal that balances smoky undertones with sweet, crisp freshness.

Ingredients

  • Sweet corn (canned or fresh off the cob)
  • Dry thyme
  • Green peas
  • Long-grain parboiled rice or Golden Sella Basmati rice
  • Small prawns or shrimp (peeled and deveined)
  • Curry powder
  • Carrots (finely chopped)
  • Rich chicken or turkey stock
  • Green beans or runner beans (sliced)
  • Chicken seasoning cubes
  • Vegetable oil or butter
  • White onion (sliced or diced)
  • Green bell pepper (chopped)
  • Salt
  • Cow or chicken liver (boiled and finely diced)
  • Spring onions / scallions (chopped)

Ordered List Of The INGREDIENTS

  1. 3 cups Long-grain parboiled rice (or Golden Sella Basmati rice)
  2. 3 cups Rich chicken or turkey stock (strained)
  3. 1 tbsp Curry powder (divided)
  4. 1 tsp Dried thyme (divided)
  5. 2 Chicken seasoning cubes
  6. Salt (to taste)
  7. ½ cup Vegetable oil or butter (divided)
  8. 1 cup Cow or chicken liver (boiled and finely diced)
  9. 150g Small prawns or shrimp (peeled and deveined)
  10. 1 Medium white onion (sliced or diced)
  11. ½ cup Carrots (finely chopped)
  12. ½ cup Green beans or runner beans (sliced)
  13. ½ cup Sweet corn (canned or fresh)
  14. ½ cup Green peas
  15. 1 Green bell pepper (chopped)
  16. ½ cup Spring onions / scallions (chopped)

How to Prepare Nigerian Fried Rice

  • Step 1: Prep and Wash the RiceWash the rice thoroughly in warm water 3 to 4 times to strip away excess surface starch. This ensures the grains stay separated and do not become soggy.Drain the water completely and set the rice aside.
  • Step 2: Cook the Seasoned RicePlace your main cooking pot over medium heat and add 2 tablespoons of oil or butter.Sauté half of your chopped white onions, half of the curry powder, and half of the thyme for 1 minute until highly fragrant.Pour in the washed rice and stir-fry continuously for 2 minutes to toast the grains slightly.Pour in the seasoned chicken or turkey stock. If the stock does not cover the rice, add a tiny bit of water. Add your seasoning cubes and salt to taste.Cover tightly, bring to a boil, then lower the heat. Let it simmer for 15 minutes until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is about 80% cooked (it should still have a slight bite to it).Spread the rice onto a wide tray to cool completely.
  • Step 3: Sauté the Proteins and VegetablesPlace a large frying pan, skillet, or wok over medium-high heat and add 2 more tablespoons of oil.Sauté the remaining white onions for 1 minute. Toss in the prawns and diced liver, stir-frying for 2–3 minutes until the prawns turn pink. Remove the liver and prawns and set them aside.In that same oily pan, add your carrots and green beans first. Stir-fry for 2 minutes.Add the sweet corn, peas, green bell pepper, the remaining curry powder, and thyme. Season with a tiny pinch of salt or seasoning cube. Stir-fry for 2 more minutes so they stay bright and crunchy.Return the liver and prawns back into the vegetable mixture, stir together, and turn off the heat.
  • Step 4: Fry in Batches (The Secret to Success)Set a clean pan or your wok over high heat.Add a small spoonful of vegetable oil, then add a few scoops of your cooled, cooked rice and a few scoops of your vegetable/meat mixture.Stir-fry vigorously together for 3 minutes. Frying in small batches ensures the rice actually "fries" and toasted smoky flavors develop instead of steaming.Throw in a handful of fresh spring onions right at the final minute of frying each batch. Repeat this batch process until all your rice and vegetables are combined.
